\section{Force Distribution in a Spherical Joint}

\begin{frame}{Problem Statement and Context}

\textbf{Context:}

A biomechanics team models a spherical joint system with radius \(6.5\) units, where the joint force density at a point \((x,y,z)\) is given by:

\[
I(x,y,z)=489.06x^2+489.06y^2+489.06z^2
\]

The objective is to compute the \textbf{total joint force} in the spherical system using spherical coordinates.

\vspace{0.4cm}

The joint force density is
\[ I(x,y,z) = 489.06 (x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2}) \]
inside the sphere of radius $R = 6.5$.

In spherical coordinates: $x = \rho \sin\phi \cos\theta$, $y = \rho \sin\phi \sin\theta$, $z = \rho \cos\phi$, and $dV = \rho^{2} \sin\phi \, d\rho \, d\phi \, d\theta$.

\[
\iiint I \, dV = 489.06 \int_{0}^{2\pi} d\theta \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin\phi \, d\phi \int_{0}^{6.5} \rho^{4} \, d\rho
\]

\[
\int_{0}^{2\pi} d\theta = 2\pi, \quad \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin\phi \, d\phi = 2, \quad \int_{0}^{6.5} \rho^{4} \, d\rho = \frac{(6.5)^{5}}{5}
\]

Final result (rounded to 4 decimal places):
\[
\boxed{14261617.5668}
\]
\end{frame}
