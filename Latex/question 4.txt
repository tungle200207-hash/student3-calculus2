\section{Question 4 — Jacobian Transformation}

\begin{frame}{Problem Statement and Context}

\textbf{Context:}

In a multivariable musculoskeletal model, the force output density function for a limb system is:

\[
P(x,y,z)=791.06xyz
\]

where \(x,y,z\) represent normalized inputs for muscle contraction, joint pressure, and bone displacement.

The transformation to new coordinates is defined by:

\[
x=\frac{u+3v+w}{4}
\]

\[
y=\frac{u-v+w}{4}
\]

\[
z=\frac{u-v-3w}{4}
\]

The region is bounded by:

\[
0\le u\le 4,
\qquad
-1\le v\le 3,
\qquad
-1\le w\le 6
\]

The objective is to compute the total musculoskeletal force output using the Jacobian transform.

\end{frame}

%------------------------------------------------
Given:
\[ P(x,y,z) = 791.06 \, xyz \]
with transformation:
\[
x = \frac{u + 3v + w}{4}, \quad y = \frac{u - v + w}{4}, \quad z = \frac{u - v - 3w}{4}
\]
over $0 \leq u \leq 4$, $-1 \leq v \leq 3$, $-1 \leq w \leq 6$.

The Jacobian determinant is $\left| \frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(u,v,w)} \right| = \frac{1}{4}$.

The integral becomes:
\[
\iiint_D 791.06 \cdot xyz \cdot \frac{1}{4} \, du \, dv \, dw
\]

After substitution and evaluation:
\[
\boxed{-105182.1393}
\]
