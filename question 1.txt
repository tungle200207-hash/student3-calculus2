\section{Question 1: Muscle Force Density in a 3D Region}

\begin{frame}{Problem Statement and Context}

\textbf{Context:}

A biomechanics researcher analyzes a 3D musculoskeletal region where the muscle force density is given by:

\[
g(x,y,z)=541.64x^6y^8z^7
\]

over the region bounded by:

\[
0\le x\le 6,
\qquad
0\le y\le 3,
\qquad
0\le z\le 5
\]

The objective is to compute the \textbf{total muscle force distributed} in the region using a triple integral.

\vspace{0.4cm}

The total force is
\[
\iiint_R g(x,y,z) \, dV = 541.64 \int_{0}^{6} x^{6} \, dx \int_{0}^{3} y^{8} \, dy \int_{0}^{5} z^{7} \, dz
\]

Compute each integral:
\[
\int_{0}^{6} x^{6} \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^{7}}{7} \right]_{0}^{6} = \frac{6^{7}}{7} = \frac{279936}{7}
\]
\[
\int_{0}^{3} y^{8} \, dy = \left[ \frac{y^{9}}{9} \right]_{0}^{3} = \frac{3^{9}}{9} = \frac{19683}{9} = 2187
\]
\[
\int_{0}^{5} z^{7} \, dz = \left[ \frac{z^{8}}{8} \right]_{0}^{5} = \frac{5^{8}}{8} = \frac{390625}{8}
\]

Final result (rounded to 4 decimal places):
\[
\boxed{2313077972464286.0000}
\]


\end{frame}
