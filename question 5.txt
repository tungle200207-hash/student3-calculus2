\section{Question 5 — Force Cost in a Tetrahedral Region}

\begin{frame}{Problem Statement and Context}

\textbf{Context:}

The following triple integral represents the force cost function in a bounded musculoskeletal system with quadratic variation along the vertical axis:

\[
\iiint_E (258.70x+369.81y+z^2)\,dV
\]

The solid tetrahedral region \(E\) is bounded by:

\[
x=0.1,
\qquad
y=0.4,
\qquad
z=0.3,
\qquad
x+y+z=4
\]

The objective is to evaluate the \textbf{total force cost} within the system.

\vspace{0.4cm}

Evaluate
\[
\iiint_E (258.70x + 369.81y + z^{2}) \, dV
\]
where $E$ is the tetrahedron bounded by $x=0.1$, $y=0.4$, $z=0.3$, $x+y+z=4$.

Using iterated integrals:
\[
\int_{x=0.1}^{3.3} \int_{y=0.4}^{4-0.3-x} \int_{z=0.3}^{4-x-y} (258.70x + 369.81y + z^{2}) \, dz \, dy \, dx
\]

Final result (rounded to 4 decimal places):
\[
\boxed{3703.8544}
\]
